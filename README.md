# The Photo Dated Tomorrow

> Di laci meja, ada foto yang belum pernah kauambil. Di belakangnya, dengan tulisan tanganmu, tanggal besok. Di foto itu kau berdiri di jembatan yang belum pernah kaulewati, tersenyum pada seseorang di luar bingkai. Kau tidak ingat menulisnya, dan kau belum membuangnya.

## Why it sparks curiosity

The text hands the reader a small, impossible object — a photograph of a day that has not happened yet, in their own handwriting — and then refuses to explain it. Every sentence opens a new question instead of closing one: who took the photo, who is smiling just outside the frame, why is the bridge unfamiliar, and why does the reader keep something they cannot remember making? The calm is in the plain, concrete details (a drawer, a bridge, handwriting), never in an instruction to relax, so the unease lingers quietly after the last line: the reader is left holding the photo too.

## How to run

Requires Node 22 or later. No dependencies, no network access.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

`main.ts` writes exactly one deterministic text (268 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).
